\section{System Overview}

The system is intended for the management and execution of individual and group training sessions in swimming pools and other sports environments. It will support instructors in preparing training programs, managing participants, presenting visual training content, and delivering audio instructions during a session.

The solution will be implemented as a web-based client-server system. The main application and data will be centrally managed, while instructors will access the system through a web interface. During a training session, the system will interact with external audio and video equipment used in the sports environment.

\section{Key Architectural Decisions}

The following preliminary architectural decisions are made:

\begin{itemize}
    \item The system will use a client-server architecture.
    \item The main user interface will be web-based and accessible from standard computers and tablets.
    \item Application data, training programs, participant information, and session history will be stored centrally.
    \item Video and other training content will be managed by the system and displayed on connected screens or TVs.
    \item Audio instructions and the instructor's voice will be delivered through suitable audio equipment, including participant headphones where applicable.
    \item The architecture should allow the same software solution to be adapted to different sports environments and hardware configurations.
\end{itemize}

\section{System Decomposition}

For project planning purposes, the system is divided into the following main parts:

\subsection{Training Program Management}

Responsible for creating, editing, storing, and managing training programs. A program consists of stages, exercises, video content, audio instructions, and other information required during a training session.

\subsection{Session Management}

Responsible for planning and conducting individual and group training sessions. It manages the execution of a prepared program and allows the instructor to start, pause, continue, skip, or change stages during a session.

\subsection{Participant Management}

Responsible for storing participant information, assigning participants to training sessions, and maintaining participation history.

\subsection{Visual Content Presentation}

Responsible for presenting exercises, demonstrations, and other visual information on a TV, display, or other suitable screen. This is particularly important in swimming pools, where the instructor may not be able to demonstrate movements performed in the water or underwater.

\subsection{Audio Communication}

Responsible for delivering prerecorded audio instructions and the instructor's voice to participants. The solution should support audio equipment suitable for the particular sports environment, including headphones for participants where required.

\subsection{Data and History Management}

Responsible for storing training programs, sessions, participation information, and relevant historical data.

\section{Preliminary Hardware Requirements}

The system requires standard computing infrastructure as well as hardware used directly in the training environment.

\begin{itemize}
    \item A server or cloud environment for hosting the application, database, and training content.
    \item A computer or tablet for the instructor to access and control the system.
    \item A TV, monitor, or other display for presenting visual training content.
    \item In non-pool environments, a mobile TV/display stand may be used where a fixed screen is not available.
    \item Audio equipment for delivering instructions and the instructor's voice.
    \item Participant headphones where individual audio delivery is required.
    \item A microphone or headset for the instructor where live voice transmission is used.
    \item Network connectivity between the application and the equipment used during the training session.
\end{itemize}

For swimming pool environments, equipment located near the water must be selected according to the environmental conditions and required water-resistance level. The exact hardware models and communication technologies will be selected during detailed system design and integration testing.

\section{Future Extensions}

The architecture should allow the system to be extended in the future without requiring major changes to its core functionality. Possible extensions include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item dedicated mobile interfaces for instructors and participants;
    \item participant identification in the sports environment;
    \item a reusable library of training programs and video content;
    \item personalized training programs based on participant needs, fitness level, or previous activity;
    \item participant progress and training result analysis;
    \item operation across multiple sports facilities or sports centers.
\end{itemize}
